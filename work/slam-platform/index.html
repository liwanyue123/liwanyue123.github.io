SLAM Platform

GNSS fails exactly where you need it most: between high-rises, under a car park. This platform pairs it with fisheye and binocular vision so the position survives the gap.

Year
2023
Role
Hardware & sensor drivers
Context
with Peking University
Focus
Drivers, Sensing, GNSS

Overview

I collaborated with students from Peking University to develop a SLAM platform. The project addresses the degradation — or complete loss — of GNSS positioning accuracy caused by satellite signal blockage and multipath effects in urban high-rise areas and underground parking lots. Our answer is a positioning solution that fuses GNSS with fisheye and binocular cameras.

My role

My work was centred on the hardware. I wrote the ROS drivers for the full sensor suite — fisheye cameras, depth cameras, GNSS, radar and lidar — and designed a circuit board capable of triggering every sensor synchronously.
