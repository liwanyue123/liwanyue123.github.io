2023 · Aerial Robotics

Bouncing Quadrotor

A quadrotor that saves energy by refusing to stay in the air. A passive spring leg lets it advance in hops, with onboard sensing to find its way between them.

Year
2023
Role
Control system, end to end
Context
RAPID Lab, Sun Yat-sen University
Focus
Dynamics, Control, Quadrotor

Overview

We developed a quadrotor with a passive spring, enabling energy-efficient jumping-flying rather than continuous powered flight. The robot carries a D435i depth camera and can autonomously navigate and evade obstacles.

My role

I was responsible for developing the entire control system. The primary goal was to let the quadrotor accurately track its desired trajectory even after a significant impact with the ground.

In the process I adapted and migrated the algorithm from the quadruped domain into the aerial one — feasible because both rest on the same foundation: a single rigid body model.
