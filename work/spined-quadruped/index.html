2022 · Legged Locomotion

Spined Quadruped Robot

Most quadruped controllers treat the trunk as one rigid body. This one has a spine that moves, and an MPC formulation that knows it.

Year

Role

Overview

I developed a 15-DoF quadruped robot with three actuated spine joints. The robot can freely change its spinal posture while trotting at high speed.

The control contribution is a convex MPC approach that accounts for the movement of the spine, rather than modelling the trunk as a single rigid segment. The paper was accepted at ICRA 2023.

My role

I took on the majority of the project tasks independently, with the exception of the structural design, which was handled by an engineer — hardware, electronics, control formulation, implementation and experiments.
