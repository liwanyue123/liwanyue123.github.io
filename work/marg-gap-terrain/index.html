MARG — Risky Gap Terrains

Blind locomotion controllers fall into holes. MARG gives the policy a terrain map and asks it to choose where to put its feet.

Overview

Existing blind locomotion controllers often struggle to ensure safe, efficient traversal of risky gap terrains, which are highly complex and require the robot to perceive terrain accurately and select appropriate footholds while moving.

MARG is a deep reinforcement learning controller that integrates terrain maps with proprioception to dynamically adjust its action and improve stability. Its terrain mapping model (TMG) relies on a single LiDAR to generate accurate terrain maps, which keeps hardware deployment simple.

Results

Experiments show the MARG controller is stable and effective across risky tasks, balancing safety, stability and efficiency during locomotion.
