RM-Planner — Mobile Manipulation

Mobile manipulation in an unknown environment splits badly into “where to stand” and “how to move”. RM-Planner puts learning on the first and model-based control on the second.

Overview

RM-Planner is a planning method for mobile manipulation in unknown, complex environments. It adopts a two-layer hierarchical framework.

The low-level planner is a whole-body MPC that tracks subgoals and generates aggressive but safe joint commands across the whole manipulation process. The high-level policy is reinforcement-learned and consumes 3D point-cloud representations of the environment directly, guiding the robot toward optimal manipulation postures given current observations and the task objective.

Results

RM-Planner significantly outperforms state-of-the-art methods in extensive simulation and real-world experiments.
