About

李皖越

I am a Ph.D. student in Mechanical Engineering at the University of Hong Kong, working on legged robots — bipeds above all. Before that I took an M.S. in Artificial Intelligence at Sun Yat-sen University, and spent seven months at XPENG Robotics on the motion control for PX5, their first-generation biped.

What ties the work together is the dynamics. A quadruped with an actuated spine and a quadrotor with a spring leg look nothing alike, but both reduce to a single rigid body in contact with the world — which is why the MPC formulation written for one transferred to the other almost intact.

These days the work leans on both halves at once: trajectory optimization and whole-body MPC on the model side, imitation and reinforcement learning on the other. Most of the interesting problems live where the two meet.
